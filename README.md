## Key Insights

- Weekend demand is substantially higher than weekday demand, with
  Saturday averaging ~41.7K units compared with ~30.1K on Wednesday.
- Demand varies considerably across stores, suggesting location-specific
  forecasting is necessary.
- FOODS represents the largest product category by sales volume.
- Annual seasonality exists, with demand generally peaking in August–September.
- Event effects are heterogeneous: sporting events are associated with
  higher demand, while national events show lower average demand.
